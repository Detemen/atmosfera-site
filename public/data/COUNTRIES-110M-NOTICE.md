# Countries 110m data notice

`countries-110m.json` is the `countries-110m.json` dataset from
[world-atlas](https://github.com/topojson/world-atlas), a preprocessed TopoJSON
distribution of Natural Earth 1:110m country data.

Natural Earth data is public domain. The source asks that use of the data be
attributed to **Natural Earth**. See the [Natural Earth terms of
use](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/).

Source retrieved: `world-atlas@2` / `countries-110m.json`.
